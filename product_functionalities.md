2. Terra Planner: 

This is where Terra starts to feel magical. The user shouldn't be greeted by an empty Kanban board—they should feel like they've just hired an experienced project planner.

---

## Step 1: Project Created
User creates project or has already created project

## Step 2: AI Thinks

Instead of instantly showing a dashboard, display a short planning sequence:

> **Building your project...**
>
> ✓ Understanding project type
>
> ✓ Analyzing site characteristics
>
> ✓ Identifying construction phases
>
> ✓ Estimating dependencies
>
> ✓ Generating execution plan

This takes just a few seconds but communicates that the AI is reasoning.

---

## Step 3: AI Presents the Plan

Terra says:

> **Your project plan is ready.**
>
> Based on your project type, budget, timeline, and site analysis, I've prepared an execution roadmap.

---

## Step 4: Auto-Generated Phases

Rather than generic columns, Terra creates a structured roadmap.

### Phase 1 — Site Validation

* Review terrain analysis ✅
* Conduct geotechnical survey
* Verify utility connections
* Confirm legal boundaries

---

### Phase 2 — Design

* Architectural concept
* Structural design
* MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing)
* Drainage design

---

### Phase 3 — Approvals

* Environmental review
* Building permit application
* Fire safety approval

---

### Phase 4 — Procurement

* Material estimates
* Supplier selection
* Equipment planning

---

### Phase 5 — Construction

* Site preparation
* Foundation
* Structural works
* Roofing
* Interior finishing

---

### Phase 6 — Completion

* Quality inspection
* Client walkthrough
* Occupancy documentation

---

## Step 5: AI Explains Its Decisions

This is what makes it feel intelligent.

The user clicks **Why is this here?**

Terra replies:

> "I recommended a geotechnical survey before foundation design because the terrain analysis indicates moderate slope variation. Soil characteristics should be confirmed before structural work begins."

Or:

> "Utility verification was prioritized because nearby infrastructure appears limited, which could affect construction timelines."

Now the plan feels grounded in the site's context.

---

## Step 6: AI Highlights Priorities

Instead of showing everything equally, Terra surfaces the next critical actions.

> **Today's priorities**
>
> 1. Conduct geotechnical survey
> 2. Verify utility access
> 3. Begin architectural concept design

This reduces decision fatigue by telling the user where to focus.

---

## Step 7: Dynamic Updates

As new information arrives, the plan evolves.

For example, the user uploads a soil report.

Terra responds:

> **Project updated**
>
> • Foundation design can now begin.
> • Drainage planning moved to high priority.
> • Estimated timeline reduced by 2 weeks.

The roadmap adapts instead of remaining static.

---

## Why this is compelling

The key shift is from **project management** to **project intelligence**.

Traditional tools ask:

> "What tasks do you want to create?"

Terra asks:

> "What are you trying to build?"

Then it answers:

> "Here's the execution plan I'd recommend, based on your project goals and what I know about this site."

That transition—from a blank workspace to an AI-generated, context-aware roadmap—is the moment users realize they're using something fundamentally different from a conventional task manager.
