I think the key is to make each product answer **one question**. If every product tries to do everything, customers won't know why they exist.

A clean framework is:

* **Lens** → *What is this land?*
* **Sim** → *What should we build here?*
* **Flow** → *How do we execute and manage it?*

That creates a logical progression.

# Why these three products fit together

Each product supports a different phase of a construction project's lifecycle:

| Product        | Primary user                                       | Core question                                             | Outcome                                                                                             |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Terra Lens** | Surveyors, developers, planners                    | *What is this site?*                                      | A comprehensive understanding of the land, its opportunities, and its constraints.                  |
| **Terra Sim**  | Architects, urban planners, engineers              | *What is the best way to develop this site?*              | Intelligent planning options and data-driven recommendations before detailed design begins.         |
| **Terra Flow** | Project managers, developers, contractors, clients | *How do we execute and monitor the project successfully?* | Ongoing visibility into construction progress, risks, documentation, and stakeholder communication. |

One suggestion I'd make is to position Terra not as **"AI for construction"**, but as **"the operating system for the entire construction lifecycle."** That tells a larger story: every project begins with understanding the land (**Lens**), moves into informed planning (**Sim**), and finishes with coordinated execution (**Flow**). It also leaves plenty of room to expand the platform in the future without changing the core narrative.
